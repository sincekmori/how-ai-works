That was a long journey — well done.
This book began at the familiar screen on your phone: the chat where a question goes in and an answer comes back.
Back then, the reason a machine could speak was still magic.

## The map of the journey, one more time

Retrace the road on the map below.
Open any stretch that interests you, and what you learned there comes back.

<JourneyMap lang="en" client:visible />

Part I dove deep inside the box.
Words into numbers (vectors), closeness measured (the dot product), hesitation held as shares (probability), learning from mistakes (gradients), seeing distant words directly (attention), parts stacked high (the Transformer), raised on mountains of text, finished by preference, generating one word at a time.

Part II stepped outside the box.
The model is a counter callable over the network; it answers in fixed shapes, holds tools, connects through a common socket, finishes multi-step work in a loop, and even learns "the way we do it here" from playbooks.

## There was no magic anywhere

Everything that looked like magic was made of the honest parts this book has shown, combined.
New words will keep arriving.
But you are equipped now.
"That one is an application of that part" — you can trace it yourself.

## Onward from here

Three ways to enjoy what comes after closing this book.

One.
Watch your everyday chat with the lid-open eyes.
As the reply streams in word by word, you now know the loop spinning underneath: pick one word, append, feed it back in.
A screen you understand never looks quite the same.

Two.
Follow the references of any chapter that caught you.
At the end of each chapter sit the papers and books its ideas came from.
If this book was the entrance, those are the main text.

Three.
If you find a mistake, tell us.
This book has not been reviewed by experts.
That is exactly why your correction is the finest gift to whoever reads it next.
The reporting entrance is just below.

And with that: enjoy the rest of the journey!
