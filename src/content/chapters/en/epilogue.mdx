Thank you for the long journey.
Remember how it began.
This book started at **that familiar screen on your phone** — the chat where you ask and an answer comes back.
Back then, the reason a machine could talk was still magic.

## The journey map, one more time

Let's retrace the road we took.
Open any leg that catches your eye and recall what it taught.

<JourneyMap lang="en" client:visible />

In Part I we dove deep into the box.
We turned words into numbers (vectors), measured closeness (the dot product), held hesitation as shares (probability), learned from mistakes (the gradient), looked at faraway words (attention), stacked the parts (the Transformer), raised it on mountains of text, tuned it to taste, and generated one word at a time.

In Part II we stepped outside the box.
The model is a window you call across the net; you make it answer in a fixed shape, hand it tools, connect them in a common shape, loop it through multi-step jobs — and teach it "your way" with playbooks.

## There was no magic anywhere

What looked like magic was, all of it, built from the **humble parts** we've seen in this book.
New words will keep arriving.
But you're fine now.
"That's an application of that part" — you can trace it yourself.

## Where to go from here

Three parting ways to keep enjoying this after the book closes.

First, **watch your everyday chat with the lid-open eyes**.
When the reply streams in one word at a time, you now know the loop turning behind it — pick a word, append it, feed it back in.
A screen you understand never looks quite the same again.

Second, **follow the references of the chapters that caught you**.
At the end of each chapter sit the papers and books these ideas came from.
If this book was the doorway, those are the main hall.

Third, **if you find a mistake, please tell us**.
This book has not been reviewed by domain experts.
That is exactly why your report is the best gift to whoever reads it next.
The place to send it is right below.

So long.
Enjoy the rest of the journey!
